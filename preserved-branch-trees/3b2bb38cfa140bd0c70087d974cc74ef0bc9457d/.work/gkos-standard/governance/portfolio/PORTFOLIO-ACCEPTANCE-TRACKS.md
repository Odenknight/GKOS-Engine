## Normative GKX release track

Required evidence: the current standard, Engine, a versioned standard-derived fixture profile, and a qualifying second independent implementation. Engine-Lite is compatibility-tested only and cannot establish independence.

This track blocks only a GKX/GKOS v1.0 conformance claim or ratification. Suite S0 does not block it unless a specific standard claim depends on Suite.

## Portfolio-publication alignment track

Required evidence: accurate Suite S0 provenance and identity, KRR disposition and naming, and aligned downstream claims. Private products are excluded unless a separate disclosure decision authorizes a specific public artifact.

This track blocks publication of an aligned portfolio narrative. It does not itself authorize a normative GKX release claim.

## Decision rule

A pass or failure on one track must be reported separately and must not be used to imply a disposition on the other. This document does not make either track pass.
