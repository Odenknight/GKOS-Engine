## Remaining qualification and scope

Full current tests and exact-head hosted checks are in progress at this report
stage. No completed cross-platform qualification, 24-hour soak, physical
power-loss test, live-model quality evaluation, signing or release is claimed.
Kosmos adapter/UI and final released artifact adoption remain downstream work.
The host requires a validated snapshot/index and current authority provider;
the synthetic example is not a production authority registry.

The Node adapter retains its cooperative-vault threat model and does not claim
directory-entry durability across power loss. Unsupported or ambiguous recovery
blocks writes. Remaining limits are release gates, not acceptance waivers.
