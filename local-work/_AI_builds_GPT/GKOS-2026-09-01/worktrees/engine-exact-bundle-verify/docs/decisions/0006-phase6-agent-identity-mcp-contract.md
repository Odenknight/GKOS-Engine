## Decision

Successor-F1 is required to freeze a deterministic, manifest-bound contract pack containing strict Draft 2020-12 schemas, the 32-operation core inventory, the seven-required/16-deferred tool registry, the exact 53-code error domain, 67 unchanged official MCP vectors, and Q21's fixture-only deterministic oracle for 468 canonical non-MCP cases and 12 aliases across 16 families. The first generated oracle was rejected and does not satisfy this decision.

Q22 allocates the canonical cases as 67 canonical, 89 migration, 107 race, and 205 security cases. Aliases remain colocated with canonical targets and do not contribute to any count. The closed set includes 44 cross-table global-event matrix cases plus four preserved same-table cases, 20 exact sink-by-sensitive-class cases, Q17, Q18, exact RSR-001, and cursor-stale-before-reference-lookup precedence.

Q23 uses an exact rational accumulator: `refill_microtokens_numerator=1000000`, `refill_interval_ms=6000`, and persisted `refill_remainder_numerator` in `0..5999`. Credit is the integer quotient and the remainder carries across requests and process restarts. This is exactly one token per six seconds with no rounding drift.

The accepted predecessor and the proposal-entry source coordinate serve different purposes. The predecessor is immutable accepted historical evidence and the base for this local successor. The manifest continues to record `source_base_commit=808d875b557f4cfd2bb0addccba44d70c9748f35`, includes no predecessor-result coordinate, and cannot imply that historical accepted evidence qualifies corrected bytes.

## Security and availability boundary

`mapping.add` accepts only `agent_id`, `namespace`, and `idempotency_key` before returning `GKOS_P6_PLATFORM_UNAVAILABLE`; no adapter-dependent subject predicate is invented. Restart-stale cursor detection precedes reference lookup and uses exact RSR-001.

Synthetic fixture identifiers, clocks, bytes, credential oracles, ledgers, diffs, retry samples, and digests are deterministic test inputs only. They define neither production entropy nor runtime digest algorithms. Secret wire values remain absent from schemas, fixtures, logs, evidence, and archives.

This decision implements no authority persistence, credential processing, migration, session engine, MCP listener, `/mcp` route, hosted-green service, Effects behavior, or platform ACL behavior. Engine-Lite receives no pack, pin, listener, or availability claim.

## Consequences

Every remediated generated byte must remain deterministic, manifest-bound, inside the accepted 40-path register, and outside protected paths. Remediation must prove exact operation or raw-transport requests, executable state/physical diffs, recomputable digests, full Q17/Q18 transitions, and exact persisted rate-bucket arithmetic. Automated local generation and validation alone do not constitute independent acceptance, runtime enforcement, activation, or publication.

The second remediation's 15-test focused local result is superseded as closure evidence by independent rejection. Further remediation must reconstruct every authorized V15 FX domain from its exact tag and preimage, reject all unratified tags, validate complete typed rows including required nullable fields, bind activity/request digests to exact wire bytes, and prove the exact session, RATE, and Q18 process-B transitions before another local success claim is recorded.

Transition-proof objects are not closure by themselves. Each credential, race, and bootstrap claim must bind the exact durable rows, physical objects, event/effect chain, session prestate, request/retry bytes, response bytes, state cut, and permitted mutation. The frozen current pair passed those strengthened gates as part of the 35/35 local suite, and independent audit accepted the exact 468-canonical/12-alias census with an empty remaining-red set. This accepts local validation only; it does not claim a commit, push, merge, hosted qualification, release, activation, or publication.

Q24 closes `bootstrap.initialize` results as a `oneOf` union. `bootstrap_result` remains the unchanged COMPLETE-state terminal result; `bootstrap_handoff_result` is the exact closed 12-field OWNER_LOCATOR_STAGED result for delivered or ambiguous handoff. A lost acknowledgement or exact replay returns the durable nonsecret coordinates with `secret_revealed=false`, byte-identically, and performs no second delivery, mutation, or reveal. Its one current request and response retain the durable original `request_id` and use that identifier as `retry_of`; the original delivery is represented only by durable prestate. Errors remain the exact six-field envelope and never gain a reveal flag.

Full F2 through F5 and Lite L1 through L4 remain unavailable and deferred. Commit, push, pull request, remote workflow, release, deployment, activation, and publication require separate authority.
